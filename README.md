# NovaBank Knowledge Base

This directory contains 18 fictional NovaBank customer-support and policy documents for a portfolio RAG project. NovaBank is not a real financial institution. The documents use Indian banking terminology and ₹ currency.

## Shared ground truth

All documents use the same figures: savings minimum monthly average balance is ₹5,000 in urban areas and ₹2,000 in semi-urban/rural areas; savings interest is 3.5% per annum; current-account minimum balance is ₹25,000; NovaBank ATM withdrawals include 5 free monthly transactions and then cost ₹20 each; other-bank ATM withdrawals include 3 free monthly transactions and then cost ₹25 each; daily ATM withdrawal limit is ₹50,000; debit-card annual fee is ₹250 and is waived in the first year; debit-card replacement is ₹200; credit-card revolving interest is 3.5% per month (42% APR); IMPS costs ₹5 per transaction; NEFT is completed within 2 hours and costs ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab; RTGS is available 24/7, requires at least ₹2,00,000, and costs ₹25–₹50 by amount slab; personal-loan interest is 10.5%–16% per annum, tenure is 12–60 months, and processing fee is 1.5% of the loan amount; account closure takes 7 working days, costs nothing after 12 months, and costs ₹500 within the first 12 months; individual-account re-KYC is every 2 years and high-risk re-KYC is every 1 year; phone banking is 24/7; branches operate Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM, and close on public holidays; complaints are acknowledged within 24 hours, resolved within 7 working days, and escalated after 15 working days if unresolved.

Additional shared controls are a 30-minute beneficiary cooling period, ₹5,00,000 daily digital transfer limit, ₹2,00,000 mobile-app transfer limit, 5-working-day ATM failed-transaction refund timeline, 5 failed-login attempts causing a 30-minute lock, 10-minute session timeout, 30-day credit-card billing cycle, minimum payment of 5% of statement balance or ₹250 whichever is higher plus applicable amounts, zero customer liability for fraud reported within 24 hours of discovery subject to investigation and security compliance, phone-support target response within 60 seconds, and secure-message response within 2 working days.
