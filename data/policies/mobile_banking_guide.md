# Mobile Banking Guide

1. Purpose
1.1 This guide explains how to register for NovaBank’s mobile banking app, set up biometrics, register and de-register devices, and keep your mobile banking secure. It also gives clear steps for common problems, customer responsibilities, and escalation paths.

2. Eligibility
2.1 Any NovaBank customer with an active savings or current account and a mobile number registered with the bank can use mobile banking.
2.2 Account minimums and product facts (for reference while using mobile banking):
- Savings minimum monthly average balance: ₹5,000 urban; ₹2,000 semi-urban/rural.
- Savings interest: 3.5% per annum.
- Current account minimum balance: ₹25,000.

2.3 KYC and re-verification
- KYC re-verification: every 2 years for individual accounts; every 1 year for high-risk category accounts.

2.4 Other product figures you may need in the app:
- ATM: 5 free monthly transactions at NovaBank ATMs, then ₹20 each; other-bank ATMs 3 free monthly, then ₹25 each.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: ₹50,000.
- Debit card annual fee: ₹250, waived first year.
- Debit card replacement fee: ₹200.
- Credit card revolving interest: 3.5% per month (42% APR).
- IMPS: instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction.
- NEFT: batch processing, within 2 hours, ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab, 24/7.
- RTGS: real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000, ₹25–₹50 by amount slab, 24/7.
- Personal loan interest: 10.5%–16% per annum by credit profile.
- Personal loan tenure: 12–60 months.
- Personal loan processing fee: 1.5% of loan amount.
- Account closure: 7 working days; no fee if account older than 12 months; ₹500 if closed within 12 months.

3. App registration — step-by-step
3.1 Before you start
- Ensure your mobile number is registered with NovaBank.
- Use a phone with a supported operating system and a secure lock screen.

3.2 Registration steps
1. Download the official NovaBank app from your device app store.
2. Open the app and choose “Register / First-time user.”
3. Enter your account number and the registered mobile number, then request an OTP.
4. Enter the OTP received via SMS.
5. Create your mobile banking login (user ID) and a strong password or MPIN as prompted.
6. Optionally enable biometrics (fingerprint/face) after successful initial login.
7. Complete any on-screen identity checks. Some registrations may require an in-branch visit for verification.

3.3 After registration
- New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period before use.
- Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000; branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.

4. Biometrics and login
4.1 Enabling biometrics
- Biometrics (fingerprint or face) can be enabled after you complete app registration and log in once with your password/MPIN.
- Biometrics are device-specific and use your device’s secure hardware. The app never stores raw biometric data.

4.2 Login rules and locks
- Digital banking locks after 5 failed login attempts for 30 minutes.
- Sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity.
- If you are locked out, wait 30 minutes or use “Forgot password/MPIN” recovery options, or contact phone banking.

5. Device registration and de-registration
5.1 Registering a device
- Each device is registered during app setup using an OTP to your registered mobile number and device verification steps inside the app.
- For security, register only devices you own and keep your device lock active.

5.2 De-registering a device
- To remove a device, open the NovaBank app on another registered device and remove it from Settings > Devices, or call phone banking.
- If you cannot access another device, call phone banking 24/7 immediately to request de-registration.

5.3 Lost or stolen device
- Action steps:
  1. Immediately call phone banking (phone banking 24/7) to block mobile access and request de-registration.
  2. Change your NovaBank login password/MPIN from a secure device.
  3. If your debit card is exposed, request a block and replacement (Debit card replacement fee: ₹200).
- ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification.

6. Security best practices and customer responsibilities
6.1 Customer responsibilities
- Keep your mobile number updated with the bank.
- Do not share OTPs, passwords, MPINs, or full debit/credit card CVV codes.
- Use device locks, screen timeout, and app updates promptly.
- Report suspected fraud or unauthorized transactions immediately. Fraud reported within 24 hours of discovery receives zero customer liability, subject to investigation and customer security compliance.

6.2 App hygiene
- Install app updates from your official app store only.
- Avoid using public Wi-Fi for fund transfers; prefer mobile data or trusted private networks.
- Regularly review beneficiaries and recent transactions.

7. Fund transfers, limits and timing
7.1 Transfer methods and timings
- IMPS: instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction.
- NEFT: batch processing, within 2 hours, ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab, 24/7.
- RTGS: real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000, ₹25–₹50 by amount slab, 24/7.

7.2 Limits and holds
- New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period.
- Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000; branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.

8. Troubleshooting, support and response times
8.1 Quick fixes
- If OTP not received: ensure your mobile signal, check message blocking and restart the phone.
- If app crashes: update app, clear cache, reinstall if necessary.

8.2 Support channels and timelines
- Phone banking 24/7 (phone support target response is within 60 seconds).
- Secure messages in the app (secure-message response within 2 working days).
- Branches Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM; closed public holidays.

9. Complaints, escalation and dispute handling
9.1 Timelines
- Complaints: acknowledgment within 24 hours; resolution within 7 working days.
- If unresolved after 15 working days, escalate to the grievance officer.
- Final complaint escalation goes to the grievance officer after 15 working days, followed by an independent banking-ombudsman-style external forum where applicable.

10. Fees and key product notes
- Debit card annual fee: ₹250, waived first year.
- Debit card replacement fee: ₹200.
- Credit card revolving interest: 3.5% per month (42% APR).
