# ATM Policy

1. Purpose
1.1 This policy explains NovaBank’s ATM fees, free-transaction limits, withdrawal limits, handling of failed transactions, and safety guidance. It is written for retail customers who hold NovaBank savings or current accounts and use NovaBank or other-bank ATMs.

2. Applicability and eligibility
2.1 This policy applies to all NovaBank debit-card holders and ATM-enabled account types.
2.2 Eligibility for debit cards requires completed KYC. KYC re-verification: every 2 years for individual accounts; every 1 year for high-risk category accounts.

2.3 Required documents (examples)
- Valid photo ID (as per your KYC).
- Proof of address.
- Account number and mobile number registered with the account.

3. Fees, free limits, and related account charges
3.1 ATM transaction allowances and charges
- ATM: 5 free monthly transactions at NovaBank ATMs, then ₹20 each; other-bank ATMs 3 free monthly, then ₹25 each.
3.2 Card fees and replacement
- Debit card annual fee: ₹250, waived first year.
- Debit card replacement fee: ₹200.
3.3 Withdrawal and transfer limits
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: ₹50,000.
- Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000; branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.

4. Withdrawals, failed transactions, and refunds
4.1 Withdrawals
- You may withdraw up to the Daily ATM withdrawal limit of ₹50,000 per day, subject to available account balance and ATM cash availability.
- If you exceed the number of free monthly transactions, the per-transaction fee (see 3.1) will be debited to your account within the next statement cycle.

4.2 Failed ATM transactions
- If an ATM debits your account but cash is not dispensed, contact NovaBank immediately and provide: ATM location, date/time, card number (masked), transaction amount, and transaction reference if available.
- ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification. We may need transaction logs from the ATM operator; refunds are processed after verification.

5. Safety and fraud
5.1 At the ATM
- Inspect the ATM for skimming devices before use. Shield your PIN entry.
- Do not accept offers of assistance from strangers. Retain transaction receipts until reconciled.
- If your card is retained by an ATM, report immediately to NovaBank and block the card.

5.2 Digital security
- Digital banking locks after 5 failed login attempts for 30 minutes; sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity.
- New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period before you can transfer funds to them.

5.3 Fraud reporting and liability
- Fraud reported within 24 hours of discovery receives zero customer liability, subject to investigation and customer security compliance. Cooperate with the investigation and provide required documents.

6. Customer responsibilities
6.1 Keep your PIN confidential and change it immediately if compromised.
6.2 Monitor account activity and notify NovaBank promptly for disputes or suspected fraud.
6.3 Ensure sufficient balance for withdrawals and transaction fees.
6.4 Maintain updated contact details so time-sensitive alerts and SMS/OTP messages reach you.

7. How to report issues and escalation
7.1 Immediate action
- For failed transactions or suspected fraud, call phone banking 24/7. Phone support target response is within 60 seconds.
- You may also submit a secure message through internet banking or visit a branch during branch hours.

7.2 Response and resolution timelines
- Complaints: acknowledgment within 24 hours; resolution within 7 working days; escalate to grievance officer if unresolved after 15 working days.
- Secure-message response within 2 working days.

7.3 Escalation
- If unresolved after escalation to the grievance officer, customers may approach an independent banking-ombudsman-style external forum where applicable.

8. Practical steps when an ATM transaction fails
8.1 Immediately note date, time, ATM ID/location, amount, and any on-screen message.
8.2 Retain any printed acknowledgement or receipt.
8.3 Call phone banking 24/7 to report. Provide the details above and your contact information.
8.4 We will raise an investigation and aim to complete ATM failed-transaction refunds within 5 working days after verification.

9. Related service notes (quick reference)
- Savings minimum monthly average balance: ₹5,000 urban; ₹2,000 semi-urban/rural.
- Savings interest: 3.5% per annum.
- Current account minimum balance: ₹25,000.
- IMPS: instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction.
- NEFT: batch processing, within 2 hours, ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab, 24/7.
- RTGS: real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000, ₹25–₹50 by amount slab, 24/7.
- Credit card revolving interest: 3.5% per month (42% APR). Credit-card billing cycle is 30 days; minimum payment is 5% of statement balance or ₹250, whichever is higher, plus applicable interest, fees, and over-limit amounts.
- Personal loan interest: 10.5%–16% per annum by credit profile. Tenure: 12–60 months. Processing fee: 1.5% of loan amount.
- Account closure: 7 working days; no fee if account older than 12 months; ₹500 if closed within 12 months.

10. Branch and support hours
- Phone banking 24/7.
- Branches Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM; closed public holidays.
