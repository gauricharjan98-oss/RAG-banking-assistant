# Customer Support Policy

1. Purpose
1.1 This document explains NovaBank's customer support channels, hours, response targets, accessibility, verification steps, and service expectations. It gives practical actions customers and staff must take, plus escalation and safety notes.

2. Channels and scope
2.1 Phone banking
- Phone banking is available 24/7.
- Phone support target response is within 60 seconds.

2.2 Branches
- Branches operate Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM; closed public holidays.
- Branch service is handled during branch hours.

2.3 Digital channels
- Mobile app and internet banking: access 24/7 subject to scheduled maintenance.
- Secure messages in the digital channels: secure-message response within 2 working days.
- Digital sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity. Digital banking locks after 5 failed login attempts for 30 minutes.

2.4 ATMs and card services
- ATM services follow the stated transaction and withdrawal limits in section 5.
- Debit card annual fee: ₹250, waived first year.
- Debit card replacement fee: ₹200.

2.5 Payment systems
- IMPS: instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction.
- NEFT: batch processing, within 2 hours, ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab, 24/7.
- RTGS: real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000, ₹25–₹50 by amount slab, 24/7.

3. Response and resolution targets
3.1 General targets
- Complaints: acknowledgment within 24 hours; resolution within 7 working days.
- If a complaint is unresolved after 15 working days it will escalate to the grievance officer.
- Final complaint escalation goes to the grievance officer after 15 working days, followed by an independent banking-ombudsman-style external forum where applicable.

3.2 Special response commitments
- Fraud reported within 24 hours of discovery receives zero customer liability, subject to investigation and customer security compliance.
- ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification.

4. Verification, privacy and security checks
4.1 Verification methods
- For phone and branch interactions we will ask for customer identity verification (registered mobile number, PIN/password, OTP, or in-branch photo ID).
- For secure digital requests we use OTP, device recognition, or registered credentials.

4.2 New beneficiary and transfer safety
- New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period before transfers to that beneficiary are permitted.
- Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000.
- Branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.

4.3 Login and session security
- Digital banking locks after 5 failed login attempts for 30 minutes.
- Sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity.
- Customers should not share passwords, PINs, or OTPs. NovaBank will never ask for full passwords or OTPs over phone or unsecured email.

5. Service expectations, fees and typical product figures
5.1 Deposits and balances
- Savings minimum monthly average balance: ₹5,000 urban; ₹2,000 semi-urban/rural.
- Savings interest: 3.5% per annum.
- Current account minimum balance: ₹25,000.

5.2 Cards and ATM rules
- ATM: 5 free monthly transactions at NovaBank ATMs, then ₹20 each; other-bank ATMs 3 free monthly, then ₹25 each.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: ₹50,000.
- Debit card annual fee: ₹250, waived first year; Debit card replacement fee: ₹200.
- Credit card revolving interest: 3.5% per month (42% APR).
- Credit-card billing cycle is 30 days; minimum payment is 5% of statement balance or ₹250, whichever is higher, plus applicable interest, fees, and over-limit amounts.

5.3 Loans and charges
- Personal loan interest: 10.5%–16% per annum by credit profile.
- Personal loan tenure: 12–60 months.
- Personal loan processing fee: 1.5% of loan amount.

5.4 Account closure
- Account closure: 7 working days; no fee if account older than 12 months; ₹500 if closed within 12 months.

6. Complaints, escalation and external review
6.1 How to register
- Phone: call phone banking 24/7.
- Branch: visit during branch hours.
- Digital: use secure message or complaint form in internet banking or mobile app.

6.2 What happens after filing
- We send an acknowledgment within 24 hours, note the reference number, and aim to resolve within 7 working days.
- If not resolved within 15 working days the case is escalated to the grievance officer.
- After escalation, unresolved matters may be directed to an independent banking-ombudsman-style external forum where applicable.

7. Accessibility and special assistance
7.1 Support for customers with disabilities
- Branches offer reasonable assistance on request; please call ahead to arrange support during branch hours.
- Phone banking can provide language support where available.

7.2 Alternative formats
- Customers may request statements and notices in print or digital formats as available. Contact phone banking or your branch.

8. Customer responsibilities
8.1 Security responsibilities
- Keep credentials, PINs and OTPs private. Notify NovaBank immediately of suspected fraud or unauthorized transactions.
- Report fraud within 24 hours to qualify for zero customer liability subject to investigation and customer security compliance.

8.2 Transfer and access responsibilities
- Verify beneficiary details before creating or approving transfers. New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period.
- Observe daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000 and mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000.

8.3 Information accuracy
- Keep KYC up to date. KYC re-verification: every 2 years for individual accounts; every 1 year for high-risk category accounts.

9. Practical steps: contacting, reporting, and follow-up
9.1 To report fraud or unauthorized activity
- Call phone banking 24/7 immediately and follow instructions.
- Follow up via secure message or branch with your complaint reference.

9.2 To add a beneficiary or make a transfer
- Add beneficiary via app or netbanking. Wait 30 minutes cooling period before transferring.
- For urgent large transfers, visit a branch and complete required verification; branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.

9.3 To escalate
- If your complaint is not resolved within the timelines above ask for escalation; unresolved items after 15 working days are forwarded to the grievance officer.

10. Safety notes and common scenarios
- ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification.
- Digital banking locks after repeated failed logins; if locked, wait 30 minutes or contact phone banking.
