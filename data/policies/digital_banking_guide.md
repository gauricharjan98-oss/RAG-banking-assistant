# Digital Banking Guide

1. Purpose
This guide explains how to register for and use NovaBank internet banking, how to reset access, and best practices for security, lockout, timeout, and safe access. It also lists key transaction limits, charges and customer responsibilities so you can use digital services confidently.

2. Eligibility
You may register for internet banking if you hold an active NovaBank savings or current account and your KYC is up to date. Savings minimum monthly average balance: ₹5,000 urban; ₹2,000 semi-urban/rural. Current account minimum balance: ₹25,000.

2.1 Required Documents
- Valid photo ID and address proof as per your KYC record.
- Active mobile number and email registered with your account.
- Debit card and/or ATM PIN (for one-time activation) if prompted.

3. How to register for Internet Banking (step-by-step)
1. Visit NovaBank official internet-banking site or download the NovaBank mobile app.
2. Click Register / First-time User.
3. Enter your account number, registered mobile number, and the OTP sent to that number.
4. Create a username and a secure password. Follow on-screen password rules.
5. If prompted, enter debit card details for verification.
6. Complete any one-time activation steps; you will receive confirmation when registration is successful.
7. For additional services (fund transfers, bill pay), you may be asked to set or confirm transaction limits.

4. Passwords, Resets and Lockout
- Use a strong password and do not share it. NovaBank will never ask for your full password or OTP by phone or email.
- If you forget your password, select Forgot Password on the login page and follow the verification steps (OTP to registered mobile/email, or debit card verification).
- Digital banking locks after 5 failed login attempts for 30 minutes; sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity.
- If locked, wait 30 minutes and try again, or use Forgot Password to reset immediately after the lock period if you prefer.
- If you suspect account compromise, contact phone banking immediately (24/7).

5. Session Timeout and Safe Access
- Sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity. Save work and log out when finished.
- Only use trusted devices and networks. Avoid public Wi‑Fi for sensitive transactions.
- Keep your device OS and app updated. Use screen lock, antivirus and app-store updates.
- Never share OTPs, PINs, passwords, or CVV. NovaBank will not request these over unsolicited calls or messages.

6. Beneficiaries, Cooling Period, and Transfer Limits
- New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period before first use.
- Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000; branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.
- IMPS: instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction.
- NEFT: batch processing, within 2 hours, ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab, 24/7.
- RTGS: real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000, ₹25–₹50 by amount slab, 24/7.
- For large or cross-border transfers, follow any additional verification steps shown during transaction.

7. ATM, Cards and Transaction Rules
- ATM: 5 free monthly transactions at NovaBank ATMs, then ₹20 each; other-bank ATMs 3 free monthly, then ₹25 each.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: ₹50,000.
- Debit card annual fee: ₹250, waived first year.
- Debit card replacement fee: ₹200.
- Credit-card revolving interest: 3.5% per month (42% APR).
- Credit-card billing cycle is 30 days; minimum payment is 5% of statement balance or ₹250, whichever is higher, plus applicable interest, fees, and over-limit amounts.

8. Account Maintenance, KYC and Closures
- Savings interest: 3.5% per annum.
- KYC re-verification: every 2 years for individual accounts; every 1 year for high-risk category accounts.
- Account closure: 7 working days; no fee if account older than 12 months; ₹500 if closed within 12 months.

9. Fraud, Refunds and Support
- If you detect fraud, report immediately. Fraud reported within 24 hours of discovery receives zero customer liability, subject to investigation and customer security compliance.
- ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification.
- Support: phone banking 24/7; branches Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM; closed public holidays.
- Phone support target response is within 60 seconds; secure-message response within 2 working days.
- Keep records (screenshots, transaction IDs, dates) to help investigations.

10. Complaints and Escalation
- Complaints: acknowledgment within 24 hours; resolution within 7 working days; escalate to grievance officer if unresolved after 15 working days.
- If unresolved after escalation, you may approach an independent banking-ombudsman-style external forum where applicable.

11. Customer Responsibilities and Safety Checklist
- Keep your registered contact details current so OTPs, alerts, and banking notices reach you.
- Log out after each session; do not save passwords on public devices.
- Use two-factor authentication where available; enable app-based biometric login if supported.
- Verify beneficiary details before confirming fund transfers; keep transaction limits in mind.
- Cooperate fully in fraud investigations; timely reporting increases chance of zero liability.

12. Practical Recovery Steps (if access lost)
1. Try Forgot Password flow online or in-app.
2. If account locked after failed attempts, wait 30 minutes and retry.
3. If compromise suspected, call phone banking immediately (24/7) to block access and cards.
4. Visit a branch during branch hours if in-person verification or document submission is required.
