# Savings Account Policy

1. Purpose
1.1 This document explains NovaBank savings account variants, eligibility, balances, interest, required documents, opening steps, features, customer responsibilities, fees, and escalation processes in clear, practical terms.

2. Key figures (ground truth)
- Savings minimum monthly average balance: ₹5,000 urban; ₹2,000 semi-urban/rural.
- Savings interest: 3.5% per annum.
- Current account minimum balance: ₹25,000.
- ATM: 5 free monthly transactions at NovaBank ATMs, then ₹20 each; other-bank ATMs 3 free monthly, then ₹25 each.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: ₹50,000.
- Debit card annual fee: ₹250, waived first year.
- Debit card replacement fee: ₹200.
- Credit card revolving interest: 3.5% per month (42% APR).
- IMPS: instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction.
- NEFT: batch processing, within 2 hours, ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab, 24/7.
- RTGS: real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000, ₹25–₹50 by amount slab, 24/7.
- Personal loan interest: 10.5%–16% per annum by credit profile.
- Personal loan tenure: 12–60 months.
- Personal loan processing fee: 1.5% of loan amount.
- Account closure: 7 working days; no fee if account older than 12 months; ₹500 if closed within 12 months.
- KYC re-verification: every 2 years for individual accounts; every 1 year for high-risk category accounts.
- Support: phone banking 24/7; branches Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM; closed public holidays.
- Complaints: acknowledgment within 24 hours; resolution within 7 working days; escalate to grievance officer if unresolved after 15 working days.
- New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period.
- Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000; branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.
- ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification.
- Digital banking locks after 5 failed login attempts for 30 minutes; sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity.
- Credit-card billing cycle is 30 days; minimum payment is 5% of statement balance or ₹250, whichever is higher, plus applicable interest, fees, and over-limit amounts.
- Fraud reported within 24 hours of discovery receives zero customer liability, subject to investigation and customer security compliance.
- Phone support target response is within 60 seconds; secure-message response within 2 working days; branch service is handled during branch hours.
- Final complaint escalation goes to the grievance officer after 15 working days, followed by an independent banking-ombudsman-style external forum where applicable.

3. Account variants
3.1 Regular Savings: For salaried and retail customers maintaining the minimum monthly average balance stated above by location (urban or semi-urban/rural).
3.2 Basic Savings: For lower-balance customers; must maintain at least the semi-urban/rural minimum if applicable.
3.3 Senior and Minor Accounts: Special variants exist; interest rate is the same unless otherwise notified. Guardians must maintain custody responsibilities for minors.

4. Eligibility and required documents
4.1 Eligibility: Indian residents and resident individuals who can provide valid KYC and meet the bank’s risk checks.
4.2 Required Documents:
- Government-issued photo ID (as per KYC standards).
- Address proof.
- Recent passport-sized photograph.
- PAN or Form 60/61 if PAN not available.
- Proof of date of birth for minors.
4.3 KYC re-verification: every 2 years for individual accounts; every 1 year for high-risk category accounts.

5. How to open an account (practical steps)
5.1 In-branch:
- Bring required documents.
- Complete account opening form and sign terms.
- Provide a deposit to meet operational requirements and fund your account.
- Receive account number, debit card application, and online banking registration forms.
5.2 Online / Mobile:
- Complete e-KYC flow, upload documents, and verify contact details.
- New accounts opened online may require physical verification depending on risk profile.
- Note: branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.
5.3 After opening:
- Register and activate mobile and internet banking.
- If a debit card is issued: first-year annual fee is waived; subsequent years ₹250. Card replacement fee ₹200.

6. Interest, balances and transactions
6.1 Interest: Savings interest: 3.5% per annum on your savings balance.
6.2 Minimum balances: Savings minimum monthly average balance: ₹5,000 urban; ₹2,000 semi-urban/rural. Current account minimum balance: ₹25,000.
6.3 ATM and withdrawal limits:
- ATM: 5 free monthly transactions at NovaBank ATMs, then ₹20 each; other-bank ATMs 3 free monthly, then ₹25 each.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: ₹50,000.
6.4 Digital transfers and fees:
- IMPS: instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction.
- NEFT: batch processing, within 2 hours, ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab, 24/7.
- RTGS: real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000, ₹25–₹50 by amount slab, 24/7.
- New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period.
- Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000.
6.5 Other product notes:
- Credit-card revolving interest: 3.5% per month (42% APR); billing cycle is 30 days; minimum payment is 5% of statement balance or ₹250, whichever is higher.
- Personal loan: interest 10.5%–16% per annum by credit profile; tenure 12–60 months; processing fee 1.5% of loan amount.

7. Fees, closures, refunds and timelines
7.1 Account closure: Closure processed in 7 working days; no fee if account older than 12 months; ₹500 if closed within 12 months.
7.2 ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification.
7.3 Digital security: Digital banking locks after 5 failed login attempts for 30 minutes; sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity.

8. Customer responsibilities and safety
8.1 Maintain required monthly average balance to avoid service restrictions.
8.2 Keep contact details up to date for OTPs and alerts.
8.3 Do not share OTPs, PINs, or full login credentials. NovaBank will never ask for your PIN/OTP over phone/email.
8.4 Report suspected fraud immediately. Fraud reported within 24 hours of discovery receives zero customer liability, subject to investigation and customer security compliance.

9. Support and complaints
9.1 Support: phone banking 24/7 (target response within 60 seconds); branches Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM; closed public holidays. Secure-message response within 2 working days.
