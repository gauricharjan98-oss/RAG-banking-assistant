# Loan FAQ

1. Purpose
1.1 This FAQ gives a concise, practical overview of NovaBank loan products (personal, home, vehicle, education) and points you to where to find full policy details. It covers eligibility, documents, key rates/fees, how to apply, repayment basics, security and escalation steps.

2. Loan types covered
2.1 Personal loans — unsecured credit for consumption or repayment of other debt.
2.2 Home loans — secured lending for purchase, construction or renovation of residential property.
2.3 Vehicle loans — secured lending for new or used two- or four-wheelers.
2.4 Education loans — financing for higher education, domestic or international, including living costs where eligible.

3. Eligibility and required documents
3.1 Basic eligibility (typical)
- Resident Indian applicants with stable income or verifiable self-employment.
- Minimum and maximum age limits vary by product and co-applicant status. Check the product policy for exact age bands.
3.2 3.1 Required Documents
- Proof of identity, proof of address, recent passport-size photograph, income proof (salary slips / IT returns / bank statements), property documents for secured loans, admission/fee schedule for education loans. Specific documents vary by product and must match NovaBank KYC. KYC re-verification: every 2 years for individual accounts; every 1 year for high-risk category accounts.

4. Key loan terms you must know
4.1 Personal loan headline numbers (standard)
- Personal loan interest: 10.5%–16% per annum by credit profile.
- Personal loan tenure: 12–60 months.
- Personal loan processing fee: 1.5% of loan amount.
4.2 Other product notes
- Home, vehicle and education loan rates and tenures depend on product policy, collateral, and credit profile. See the specific product policy page for current offered rates and maximum tenures.
4.3 Related account and card figures you may need
- Savings minimum monthly average balance: ₹5,000 urban; ₹2,000 semi-urban/rural.
- Savings interest: 3.5% per annum.
- Current account minimum balance: ₹25,000.
- Debit card annual fee: ₹250, waived first year.
- Debit card replacement fee: ₹200.
- Credit card revolving interest: 3.5% per month (42% APR).
- Credit-card billing cycle is 30 days; minimum payment is 5% of statement balance or ₹250, whichever is higher, plus applicable interest, fees, and over-limit amounts.

5. How to apply — practical steps
5.1 Online application (fastest)
- Log into NovaBank internet banking or mobile app, choose loan product, complete online form, upload required documents.
- New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period before funds can be sent to a new beneficiary.
- Mobile app transfers are subject to mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000; daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000.
5.2 Branch or phone application
- Call phone banking 24/7 or visit a branch. Branches are open Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM; closed public holidays. Branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.
5.3 Verification and sanction
- We verify documents, run credit checks and valuation for secured loans. Expect status updates via secure message and phone.

6. Disbursal, transfers and practical limits
6.1 Transfer methods and charges
- IMPS: instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction.
- NEFT: batch processing, within 2 hours, ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab, 24/7.
- RTGS: real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000, ₹25–₹50 by amount slab, 24/7.
6.2 ATM and card limits
- ATM: 5 free monthly transactions at NovaBank ATMs, then ₹20 each; other-bank ATMs 3 free monthly, then ₹25 each.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: ₹50,000.
- ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification.

7. Repayment, prepayment and account matters
7.1 Repayment set-up
- Repayments can be auto-debited from a linked NovaBank account or paid manually online/at branch. Auto-debit requires valid mandate and cleared balance.
7.2 Prepayment and foreclosure
- Terms depend on loan type. Check the product policy for prepayment charges where applicable.
7.3 Account closure and fees
- Account closure: 7 working days; no fee if account older than 12 months; ₹500 if closed within 12 months.

8. Security, fraud and customer responsibilities
8.1 Digital security
- Digital banking locks after 5 failed login attempts for 30 minutes; sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity.
8.2 Fraud reporting and liability
- Fraud reported within 24 hours of discovery receives zero customer liability, subject to investigation and customer security compliance.
8.3 Your responsibilities
- Keep documents and login credentials secure, notify NovaBank immediately of suspected fraud, maintain required minimum balances, and ensure timely EMIs to avoid credit consequences.

9. Complaints, response targets and escalation
9.1 Response timelines
- Phone support target response is within 60 seconds; secure-message response within 2 working days; branch service is handled during branch hours.
- Complaints: acknowledgment within 24 hours; resolution within 7 working days.
9.2 Escalation
- If unresolved after 15 working days, escalate to the grievance officer. Final complaint escalation goes to the grievance officer after 15 working days, followed by an independent banking-ombudsman-style external forum where applicable.

10. Where to find full policy documents
10.1 Product policies and full terms are available on the NovaBank website and at branches. For rate cards, fee schedules, and detailed eligibility matrices consult the specific loan product policy page or request a printed copy at any branch.

Document control
NovaBank may update this document from time to time. Customers should use the latest published version available on the NovaBank website or at branches.
