# Account Closure Policy

1. Purpose
1.1 This document explains how customers can close NovaBank deposit and current accounts, the required steps and forms, timelines, applicable fees, handling of balances and dues, records that customers should keep, and how to escalate unresolved issues.

2. Eligibility
2.1 Any account holder may request closure of their NovaBank savings or current account provided all account obligations are settled and KYC is up to date.
2.2 KYC re-verification: every 2 years for individual accounts; every 1 year for high-risk category accounts.

2.1 Required Documents
- Original account holder photo ID (as per bank KYC).
- Original PAN or Form 60/61 where applicable.
- Account passbook or latest statement, and cheque-book/debit card if available.
- Signed Account Closure Request Form (available at branch or via secure message on digital banking).
- For joint accounts: signature(s) as required by the mandate.
- For third-party requests (legal heirs, power of attorney): certified documents as per branch verification.

3. How to Request Closure
3.1 In-branch (preferred for fastest resolution)
- Visit any NovaBank branch during branch hours (Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM; closed public holidays).
- Present required documents and complete the Account Closure Request Form.
- Return unused cheque-books and debit/credit cards physically if requested.
- Provide written instructions for transfer or cheque issuance of final balance.

3.2 Phone or Secure Message
- Phone banking is available 24/7 and can initiate a closure request after verifying your identity; you may be asked to visit a branch to finalize paperwork.
- Secure-message response target is within 2 working days. The bank will advise the next steps and documents required.

3.3 Mobile App / Internet Banking
- You may initiate closure through the secure messaging channel or closure workflow where available. New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period; this does not affect closure but affects transfer of final funds if adding new beneficiary.

4. Timelines and Fees
4.1 Account closure processing time: 7 working days from receipt of a complete request and required documents.
4.2 Account closure fee:
- No fee if account older than 12 months.
- ₹500 if closed within 12 months.
4.3 Final credit of residual balance: transfers to existing verified accounts or issuing a cheque will be completed as per banking channel limits and timelines.

5. Handling Balances, Dues and Linked Services
5.1 Final balance options
- Transfer to another NovaBank account or external account (subject to daily digital fund-transfer limit of ₹5,00,000; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000).
- Issue a bank cheque payable to the account holder.
- Cash withdrawal within branch cash handling limits.

5.2 Outstanding dues
- All overdrafts, unpaid cheque returns, unpaid EMIs, loan dues and credit-card outstanding must be cleared before closure.
- Credit cards: any outstanding balance will attract credit card revolving interest of 3.5% per month (42% APR) until settled.
- Personal loans: outstanding balances must be repaid as per loan terms (personal loan interest: 10.5%–16% per annum by credit profile; tenure 12–60 months; processing fee 1.5% of loan amount where applicable on new loans).
- If you have fixed deposits or recurring deposits linked, indicate whether to close them prematurely or retain them; premature FD closure follows the FD terms.

5.3 Standing instructions, auto-debits, and beneficiaries
- Cancel standing instructions and scheduled payments before closure.
- Note: new beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period; transfers to new beneficiaries may be delayed.
- IMPS is instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction. NEFT is batch processing, within 2 hours, ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab, 24/7. RTGS is real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000, ₹25–₹50 by amount slab, 24/7.

6. Returned / Uncollected Items and Chargebacks
- Unpresented cheques, pending inward credits, or disputes must be resolved prior to closure. NovaBank will not close accounts with unresolved chargebacks or fraud investigations.
- ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification.

7. Records, Certificates and Final Account Statement
- On closure, NovaBank will provide a final account statement and a Closure Confirmation letter.
- Customers should take physical or digital copies of final statements and closure confirmation.
- NovaBank retains internal records to meet compliance requirements; customers should retain their own records.

8. Customer Responsibilities
- Ensure all dues, EMI/loan repayments, credit card balances, and overdrafts are cleared before requesting closure.
- Return unused cheque-books and debit/credit cards when requested. Debit card annual fee is ₹250, waived first year; debit card replacement fee is ₹200.
- Confirm beneficiary details and transfer limits before initiating final transfers. Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000. Branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.
- Protect digital banking credentials: digital banking locks after 5 failed login attempts for 30 minutes; sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity.
- Report suspected fraud immediately. Fraud reported within 24 hours of discovery receives zero customer liability, subject to investigation and customer security compliance.

9. Escalation, Complaints and Safety Notes
- If you have a complaint about the closure process, NovaBank will acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 7 working days.
- If unresolved after 15 working days, escalate to the NovaBank grievance officer. Final complaint escalation goes to the grievance officer after 15 working days, followed by an independent banking-ombudsman-style external forum where applicable.
- Phone support target response is within 60 seconds. Secure-message response within 2 working days. Branch service is handled during branch hours.
- If you suspect a fraud or unauthorized transaction, report immediately to phone banking (24/7). Prompt reporting may limit your liability.

10. Other service charges and operational limits (for reference)
- Savings minimum monthly average balance: ₹5,000 urban; ₹2,000 semi-urban/rural.
- Savings interest: 3.5% per annum.
- Current account minimum balance: ₹25,000.
- ATM: 5 free monthly transactions at NovaBank ATMs, then ₹20 each; other-bank ATMs 3 free monthly, then ₹25 each.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: ₹50,000.
