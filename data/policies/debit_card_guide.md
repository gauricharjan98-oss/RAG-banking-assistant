# Debit Card Guide

1. Purpose
1.1 This guide explains NovaBank debit card issuance, activation, PIN management, fees, replacement and blocking procedures, transaction limits, and safe-use responsibilities. It is written for individual and business customers who hold NovaBank savings or current accounts.

2. Eligibility and linked accounts
2.1 Eligibility
- Debit cards are issued to verified account holders whose account status is active and KYC-compliant.
- KYC re-verification: every 2 years for individual accounts; every 1 year for high-risk category accounts.

2.2 Required documents
- Valid photo ID, address proof, and PAN or other tax identifier as required by KYC rules.
- For minors or joint accounts, additional documents or guardianship paperwork may be required. Ask your branch for specifics.

2.3 Minimum balances and linked account notes
- Savings minimum monthly average balance: ₹5,000 urban; ₹2,000 semi-urban/rural.
- Current account minimum balance: ₹25,000.
- Benefits such as interest are paid on savings at 3.5% per annum.

3. Issuance and delivery
3.1 How to apply
- Apply at your branch during branch hours (Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM; closed public holidays) or via phone banking/secure message where available.
- New physical cards are mailed to the registered address after verification.

3.2 Fees at issuance
- Debit card annual fee: ₹250, waived first year.
- Fee is charged to the linked account on first annual renewal after the waiver period.

4. Activation and PIN
4.1 Activation steps
- On receiving your card, activate by calling phone banking, using the mobile app, or visiting any NovaBank ATM. Follow on-screen or voice prompts to complete activation.
- For mobile-app activations, ensure your mobile number and device are registered.

4.2 PIN setup and changes
- You will be given a PIN-on-receipt or you may set a PIN at an ATM or branch.
- Change your PIN immediately if you suspect compromise. Never write your PIN on the card or keep it with the card.

4.3 Digital security notes
- Digital banking locks after 5 failed login attempts for 30 minutes; sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity.
- Do not share OTPs, PINs, or full card details with anyone claiming to be bank staff.

5. Fees, transaction limits, and payment channels
5.1 ATM transactions and cash limits
- ATM: 5 free monthly transactions at NovaBank ATMs, then ₹20 each; other-bank ATMs 3 free monthly, then ₹25 each.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: ₹50,000.
- ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification.

5.2 Digital fund transfers and timings
- Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000; branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.
- New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period.
- IMPS: instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction.
- NEFT: batch processing, within 2 hours, ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab, 24/7.
- RTGS: real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000, ₹25–₹50 by amount slab, 24/7.

5.3 Other relevant finance figures (for customer context)
- Credit-card revolving interest: 3.5% per month (42% APR).
- These are provided for reference where debit-to-credit interactions occur.

6. Replacement, blocking and lost/stolen cards
6.1 Immediate steps if card is lost or stolen
- Block your card immediately via phone banking (phone banking 24/7) or the mobile app. Phone support target response is within 60 seconds.
- If unable to reach phone support, visit your branch during branch hours.

6.2 Replacement and fees
- Debit card replacement fee: ₹200.
- Replacement cards are issued after identity verification. Delivery times vary by location.

6.3 Liability and fraud
- Fraud reported within 24 hours of discovery receives zero customer liability, subject to investigation and customer security compliance.
- If you report later than 24 hours, liability will be assessed per bank investigation and customer adherence to security responsibilities.

7. Customer responsibilities and safe use
7.1 Your responsibilities
- Keep card and PIN secure; do not share OTP, PIN, or full card details.
- Maintain correct contact details (phone, email, address) for activation, alerts, and dispute handling.
- Immediately report suspicious transactions or card loss.

7.2 Safe transaction practices
- Verify merchant and ATM integrity before use.
- Use only secure networks when accessing digital banking.
- Confirm beneficiary details carefully; remember new fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period.

8. Disputes, refunds and escalation
8.1 Dispute handling and timelines
- Complaints: acknowledgment within 24 hours; resolution within 7 working days; escalate to grievance officer if unresolved after 15 working days.
- Secure-message response within 2 working days.

8.2 Escalation path
- Start with phone banking or branch.
- If unresolved after 15 working days, the matter is escalated to the grievance officer, and thereafter to an independent banking-ombudsman-style external forum where applicable.

9. Additional operational notes
- Account closure: 7 working days; no fee if account older than 12 months; ₹500 if closed within 12 months.
- KYC re-verification and branch/phone support details as listed above apply to card-related servicing.
