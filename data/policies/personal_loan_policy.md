# Personal Loan Policy

1. Purpose
1.1 This document explains NovaBank’s personal loan product, who can apply, the documentation and verification we require, fees and rates, repayment and prepayment options, customer responsibilities, and how to escalate service issues. It is written for customers and front-line staff; it is not legal boilerplate.

2. Eligibility
2.1 Basic eligibility
- Resident individuals aged 21–65 at loan maturity.
- Minimum net monthly income and stable employment or self-employment history as assessed by NovaBank.
- Credit profile acceptable to NovaBank; applicants are placed into credit bands that determine pricing and approval.
2.2 Account relationship
- An existing NovaBank savings or current account is recommended for faster disbursal and repayments. See section 9 for related account figures.

3. Required documents
3.1 Primary documents (submit copies and originals for verification)
- Proof of identity (government photo ID).
- Proof of address (utility bill / rental agreement / official letter).
- Proof of income: latest salary slips (last 3 months) for salaried applicants or last 2 years’ ITR and bank statements for self-employed applicants.
- Bank statements: last 6 months for salary credit and financial review.
3.2 KYC and re-verification
- KYC re-verification is required every 2 years for individual accounts; every 1 year for high-risk category accounts.

4. Loan terms, rates and fees
4.1 Interest rate and tenure
- Personal loan interest: 10.5%–16% per annum by credit profile.
- Personal loan tenure: 12–60 months.
4.2 Processing fee
- Personal loan processing fee: 1.5% of loan amount. This fee is collected at sanction or disbursal as stated in your sanction letter.
4.3 Other charges
- All other product charges applicable to linked accounts and services (see section 9) will apply.

5. Repayment, prepayment and defaults
5.1 Repayment
- Repayment will typically be by equated monthly installments (EMIs) from your NovaBank account or another designated account per the sanction letter.
5.2 Prepayment
- You may prepay in part or full. Prepayment terms and any charges, if applicable, will be disclosed in your sanction letter and account agreement.
5.3 Defaults and late payment
- Timely repayment is your responsibility. Late or missed payments may affect your credit profile and may attract interest and fees as disclosed in the sanction letter.

6. Application, approval and disbursal steps
6.1 How to apply
- Apply online via NovaBank mobile app or website, by phone banking, or at any branch during branch hours.
6.2 Verification and approval
- We verify documents, income, and credit profile. Additional checks (employment/field verification) may be done at bank discretion.
6.3 Disbursal
- On approval and completion of documentation, disbursal will be to your designated account. For new beneficiaries and transfers, note that new fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period.
6.4 Practical timeline
- Expect application acknowledgement immediately and phone banking support 24/7 for status. Final verification timelines vary by case.

7. Customer responsibilities and security
7.1 Customer responsibilities
- Provide true and complete documents and information; update the bank for any material changes in employment, income, or address.
- Maintain sufficient balance for scheduled EMI debits.
7.2 Security and digital access
- Digital banking locks after 5 failed login attempts for 30 minutes; sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity.
- Report suspected fraud immediately. Fraud reported within 24 hours of discovery receives zero customer liability, subject to investigation and customer security compliance.
7.3 Fund-transfer limits
- Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000; branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.

8. Support, complaints and escalation
8.1 Support channels and hours
- Phone banking 24/7; branches Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM; closed public holidays.
- Phone support target response is within 60 seconds; secure-message response within 2 working days; branch service is handled during branch hours.
8.2 Complaints handling
- Complaints: acknowledgment within 24 hours; resolution within 7 working days.
- If unresolved after 15 working days, escalate to the grievance officer. Final complaint escalation goes to the grievance officer after 15 working days, followed by an independent banking-ombudsman-style external forum where applicable.
8.3 Escalation steps (practical)
- Step 1: Contact phone banking or branch. Get acknowledgement/reference number.
- Step 2: If unresolved within 7 working days, follow up with secure message or branch manager.
- Step 3: If unresolved after 15 working days, request escalation to the grievance officer.

9. Related service figures (reference)
- Savings minimum monthly average balance: ₹5,000 urban; ₹2,000 semi-urban/rural.
- Savings interest: 3.5% per annum.
- Current account minimum balance: ₹25,000.
- ATM: 5 free monthly transactions at NovaBank ATMs, then ₹20 each; other-bank ATMs 3 free monthly, then ₹25 each.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: ₹50,000.
- Debit card annual fee: ₹250, waived first year.
- Debit card replacement fee: ₹200.
- Credit card revolving interest: 3.5% per month (42% APR).
- IMPS: instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction.
- NEFT: batch processing, within 2 hours, ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab, 24/7.
- RTGS: real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000, ₹25–₹50 by amount slab, 24/7.
- ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification.
- New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period.
- Digital banking locks after 5 failed login attempts for 30 minutes; sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity.
- Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000.
- Account closure: 7 working days; no fee if account older than 12 months; ₹500 if closed within 12 months.
- KYC re-verification: every 2 years for individual accounts; every 1 year for high-risk category accounts.

10. Safety notes
- Never share OTPs, passwords or banking credentials. NovaBank will never ask for your full password or OTP over phone or email.
- Use strong passwords and update contact details promptly to receive transactional alerts.
