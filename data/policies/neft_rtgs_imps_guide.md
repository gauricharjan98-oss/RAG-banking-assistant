# NEFT/RTGS/IMPS Guide

1. Purpose
1.1 This guide explains how NEFT, RTGS and IMPS transfers work at NovaBank, compares processing times, fees and minimums, and tells you which channel to use for different needs. It also lists customer responsibilities, security notes and escalation steps.

2. Eligibility and account requirements
2.1 General eligibility: Any NovaBank customer with an active savings or current account may use IMPS, NEFT or RTGS subject to limits and KYC status.
2.2 Savings and current-account rules: Savings minimum monthly average balance: ₹5,000 urban; ₹2,000 semi-urban/rural. Current account minimum balance: ₹25,000.
2.3 KYC and re‑verification: KYC re-verification: every 2 years for individual accounts; every 1 year for high-risk category accounts. Transfers may be blocked if KYC is overdue.

3. Transfer types — processing, minimums, fees and availability
3.1 IMPS: IMPS: instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction. Best for immediate transfers of small or medium value at any time.
3.2 NEFT: NEFT: batch processing, within 2 hours, ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab, 24/7. Best for routine transfers where near-term settlement is acceptable.
3.3 RTGS: RTGS: real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000, ₹25–₹50 by amount slab, 24/7. Use RTGS for large-value immediate transfers (minimum ₹2,00,000).

4. Channel selection and practical steps
4.1 Mobile app (recommended for convenience)
- Choose beneficiary (or add new beneficiary). New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period.
- Select IMPS/NEFT/RTGS as available in the app. Note mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000 per transaction; the overall Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000.
- Authenticate with your app PIN/biometrics/OTP and confirm.
- Session timeout: sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity.

4.2 Internet banking (netbanking)
- Log in, go to Funds Transfer > Add Beneficiary (30-minute cooling period applies), or select an existing beneficiary.
- Choose NEFT/RTGS/IMPS based on amount and urgency.
- Complete two-factor authentication and confirm.

4.3 Branch-assisted transfers and phone banking
- Branches are open Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM; closed public holidays. Branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.
- Phone banking 24/7 can initiate transfers after voice/OTP authentication where supported.
- For large RTGS transfers initiated at a branch, bring valid ID and account verification may be required.

4.4 Channel selection summary
- Use IMPS for instant, anytime transfers (small to medium values).
- Use NEFT for routine transfers where processing within 2 hours is acceptable.
- Use RTGS for immediate high-value transfers of at least ₹2,00,000.

5. Fees and limits (summary)
5.1 IMPS: ₹5 per transaction.
5.2 NEFT: ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab.
5.3 RTGS: ₹25–₹50 by amount slab; minimum ₹2,00,000.
5.4 Daily and per-channel limits: Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000. Branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.
5.5 Other related service figures (for your reference): ATM: 5 free monthly transactions at NovaBank ATMs, then ₹20 each; other-bank ATMs 3 free monthly, then ₹25 each. Daily ATM withdrawal limit: ₹50,000.

6. Customer responsibilities and security
6.1 Keep credentials secure: Do not share login IDs, passwords, OTPs or UPI/transaction PINs. Digital banking locks after 5 failed login attempts for 30 minutes.
6.2 Verify beneficiary details: You are responsible for ensuring beneficiary account number and IFSC/identifier are correct. Incorrect details may cause loss or delay; refunds follow bank verification.
6.3 New beneficiary cooling: New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period before transfers can be made.
6.4 Fraud reporting: Fraud reported within 24 hours of discovery receives zero customer liability, subject to investigation and customer security compliance.
6.5 Session and lock behavior: Sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity. After 5 failed login attempts the account locks for 30 minutes.

7. Failures, refunds and disputes
7.1 Failed transactions and ATM refunds: ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification.
7.2 Complaint handling: Complaints: acknowledgment within 24 hours; resolution within 7 working days; escalate to grievance officer if unresolved after 15 working days. Final complaint escalation goes to the grievance officer after 15 working days, followed by an independent banking-ombudsman-style external forum where applicable.
7.3 How to escalate: If resolution is delayed, request a complaint reference number, then ask for escalation to the grievance officer after 15 working days.

8. Practical examples and tips
8.1 Urgent ₹3,00,000 transfer at 11:00 PM: Use RTGS (RTGS: real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000) or call phone banking/visit branch if preferred—RTGS is available 24/7.
8.2 Transfer ₹50,000 late night: IMPS is instant and cheaper (IMPS: instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction) and available via mobile app or netbanking.
8.3 Adding a new beneficiary: Add beneficiary via app/netbanking; wait 30 minutes before attempting the first transfer.
8.4 Large daily needs: If you need to send more than mobile-app limit, use netbanking or multiple authorized channels up to the Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000.

9. Support and related services
9.1 Contact and response targets: Phone support target response is within 60 seconds; secure-message response within 2 working days; branch service is handled during branch hours.
9.2 Related product figures: Savings interest: 3.5% per annum. Debit card annual fee: ₹250, waived first year. Debit card replacement fee: ₹200. Credit card revolving interest: 3.5% per month (42% APR). Personal loan interest: 10.5%–16% per annum by credit profile; tenure 12–60 months; processing fee 1.5% of loan amount. Account closure: 7 working days; no fee if account older than 12 months; ₹500 if closed within 12 months.

10. Escalation and safety notes
10.1 If you suspect fraudulent activity, contact NovaBank immediately via phone banking 24/7 and report within 24 hours to be eligible for zero customer liability policies (subject to investigation).
10.2 Keep copies/screenshots of transaction confirmations and complaint reference numbers for escalation.
