# Complaint Resolution Policy

1. Purpose
1.1 This policy explains how NovaBank customers can file complaints, what to expect after filing, service-level timelines, escalation steps, and external redress options. It provides practical steps, customer responsibilities, and safety notes so complaints are handled quickly and fairly.

2. Scope and Eligibility
2.1 Scope: Applies to all retail and small-business customers of NovaBank for account services, cards, payments, loans, ATM disputes, digital banking, branch services, and related fees or service failures.
2.2 Eligibility: Any customer with a NovaBank relationship can file a complaint. Basic account facts you may need to reference: Savings minimum monthly average balance: ₹5,000 urban; ₹2,000 semi-urban/rural. Savings interest: 3.5% per annum. Current account minimum balance: ₹25,000.

2.3 Relevant service figures (for reference when complaining about a specific service)
- ATM: 5 free monthly transactions at NovaBank ATMs, then ₹20 each; other-bank ATMs 3 free monthly, then ₹25 each. Daily ATM withdrawal limit: ₹50,000.
- Debit card annual fee: ₹250, waived first year. Debit card replacement fee: ₹200.
- Credit card revolving interest: 3.5% per month (42% APR). Credit-card billing cycle is 30 days; minimum payment is 5% of statement balance or ₹250, whichever is higher, plus applicable interest, fees, and over-limit amounts.
- IMPS: instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction. NEFT: batch processing, within 2 hours, ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab, 24/7. RTGS: real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000, ₹25–₹50 by amount slab, 24/7.
- Personal loan interest: 10.5%–16% per annum by credit profile. Personal loan tenure: 12–60 months. Personal loan processing fee: 1.5% of loan amount.
- New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period. Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000; branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.
- ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification.
- Account closure: 7 working days; no fee if account older than 12 months; ₹500 if closed within 12 months.
- KYC re-verification: every 2 years for individual accounts; every 1 year for high-risk category accounts.
- Digital banking locks after 5 failed login attempts for 30 minutes; sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity.
- Fraud reported within 24 hours of discovery receives zero customer liability, subject to investigation and customer security compliance.

3. Filing a Complaint — Practical Steps
3.1 Channels
- Phone banking 24/7 (target response within 60 seconds).
- Secure message via online banking or mobile app (secure-message response within 2 working days).
- Visit any branch Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM (closed public holidays). Branch service is handled during branch hours.
- Use the complaint form available at branches or via secure-message. If you need the grievance officer contact, the branch or secure-message will provide it.

3.2 What to include
- Your full name, account number, and contact details.
- Date, time, transaction reference(s), amount(s), and channel (ATM, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS, branch, mobile app).
- Copies or screenshots of receipts, SMS alerts, or transaction failures.
- Any ATM receipts or error messages (retain originals until issue resolved).

3.3 Receipt and reference
- You will receive an acknowledgment within 24 hours; note the complaint reference number for follow-up.

4. Acknowledgment and Resolution SLA
4.1 Acknowledgment within 24 hours; resolution within 7 working days; escalate to grievance officer if unresolved after 15 working days.
4.2 If a case requires more investigation, NovaBank will update you on progress and expected completion date. Where external parties (other banks, switches) are involved, timelines may include their response time, but NovaBank will keep you informed.

5. Resolution Process and Outcomes
5.1 Investigation: We verify transaction logs, CCTV (if branch/ATM), third-party confirmations, and customer's provided evidence.
5.2 Refunds and corrections:
- ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification.
- For disputed fund transfers (IMPS/NEFT/RTGS), we follow transaction rules and the above service timelines.
- Fraud: if reported within 24 hours of discovery and customer has complied with security obligations, fraud reported within 24 hours of discovery receives zero customer liability, subject to investigation and customer security compliance.
5.3 Fee disputes: We review charges against posted fee schedules (for example ATM charge rules, card fees, account closure fees) and correct errors where found.

6. Escalation and Grievance Officer
6.1 If a complaint is not resolved to your satisfaction within 15 working days, you may request escalation. Escalate in writing via secure-message or at a branch, referencing your original complaint number.
6.2 Final complaint escalation goes to the grievance officer after 15 working days, followed by an independent banking-ombudsman-style external forum where applicable.
6.3 The grievance officer will review and respond with a final internal decision and next steps.

7. Customer Responsibilities
7.1 Provide accurate information and documentation promptly.
7.2 Report suspected fraud immediately; timely reporting affects liability determination.
7.3 Protect your credentials: do not share passwords, OTPs, or PINs. If you suspect account compromise, change credentials and inform NovaBank immediately.
7.4 Cooperate with investigations, including providing additional documents when requested.

8. Safety Notes and Digital Security
8.1 Digital banking locks after 5 failed login attempts for 30 minutes; sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity. These safeguards help prevent fraud.
8.2 New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period. Understand channel limits (daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000).
8.3 If you experience repeated unauthorized attempts or suspicious activity, contact phone banking 24/7 immediately.

9. Recordkeeping and Reporting
9.1 NovaBank maintains records of complaints, investigations, and resolutions. You can request a summary of your complaint history via secure-message or at a branch.
9.2 For statutory or audit reasons, some records are retained according to internal policies.

10. Contact and Service Targets
10.1 Phone support target response is within 60 seconds. Secure-message response within 2 working days. Branches handle service during branch hours: Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM; closed public holidays.

Document control
NovaBank may update this document from time to time. Customers should use the latest published version when filing a complaint.
