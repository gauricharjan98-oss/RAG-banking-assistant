# Fraud and Security Guide

1. Purpose
1.1 This guide explains how to recognise and report fraud, NovaBank’s 24-hour zero-liability rule, common scams and phishing, and what NovaBank never asks. It gives clear, practical steps you must follow and how we will respond.

2. Scope and eligibility
2.1 This guide applies to all NovaBank individual account holders and authorised signatories. Business accounts should follow their account authority rules when reporting. KYC re-verification: every 2 years for individual accounts; every 1 year for high-risk category accounts.

2.2 Required documents for reporting
- Account number and registered mobile number.
- Transaction details: date, amount, beneficiary, transaction reference.
- Screenshot or printout of suspicious messages, emails, or screens.
- Photo ID copy and proof of address if requested.
- Police FIR copy if applicable or requested by NovaBank.

4. How to report suspected fraud (step-by-step)
4.1 Immediate actions you should take
- Call phone banking 24/7 immediately to block the card, account access, or stop transfers. Phone support target response is within 60 seconds.
- Use the mobile app to block cards or change passwords if you can do so securely.
- Preserve evidence: take screenshots, save emails/SMS, and note exact transaction IDs and timestamps.
- Do not click any links in suspicious messages. Do not reveal OTPs, passwords, or PINs.

4.2 Formal reporting
- Provide required documents (see 2.2) and a written statement if asked.
- If funds have left your account, NovaBank will begin investigation immediately. ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification.
- If you discover fraud, report it within 24 hours to be eligible for the 24-hour zero-liability rule (see section 5).

5. 24-hour zero-liability rule
- Fraud reported within 24 hours of discovery receives zero customer liability, subject to investigation and customer security compliance.
- This means we will reverse unauthorised debits where evidence supports it and you followed security steps requested by the bank (reporting, blocking, preserving evidence).
- If investigation shows customer negligence (for example, sharing OTP or PIN), liability is determined according to the facts.

6. Common scams and phishing (what to watch for)
- Phishing (email): Fake emails asking you to click a link and “re-enter” credentials. Do not click links or enter passwords from email prompts.
- Vishing (phone): Callers impersonating bank staff asking for OTP, PIN, or full card details. NovaBank will never ask for these (see section 7).
- Smishing (SMS): Messages with shortened links or urgent payment requests. Ignore and report.
- Fake payment apps or “refund” scams: Do not install unknown apps or transfer money to “safe” accounts.
- Social-engineering scams: Pressure to act now, claims of being an officer, or offers that require you to break security steps.

7. What NovaBank never asks
- NovaBank never asks you to disclose your full debit card PIN, netbanking password, or full MPIN.
- NovaBank never asks for OTPs, CVV, or password over phone, SMS, email, or social media.
- NovaBank never requests that you transfer money to another account to “reverse” a transaction or to protect your account.
- NovaBank never asks you to share secure credentials in unencrypted messages or to install third-party remote-access software.

8. Customer responsibilities
- Keep your mobile number and email updated with NovaBank.
- Use unique, strong passwords and change them regularly.
- Never share OTPs, PINs, passwords, or CVV.
- Enable transaction alerts and review statements regularly.
- Follow the 30-minute new beneficiary cooling period and check beneficiary details before transferring.
- Report suspicious activity promptly to phone banking 24/7.

9. Escalation and resolution
- We acknowledge fraud complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve within 7 working days.
- If unresolved after 15 working days, escalate to the grievance officer.
- If still unresolved, you may approach an independent banking-ombudsman-style external forum where applicable.
- For urgent blocks or freezes, phone banking 24/7 is the fastest option.
