# Fees and Charges

1. Purpose
1.1 This document consolidates NovaBank’s fees and charges in a single master schedule and explains when each fee applies. It is written for customers and support staff to ensure consistent handling and clear expectations.

2. Eligibility and basic rules
2.1 Eligibility
- All retail customers with NovaBank savings or current accounts are subject to the fees below, unless a separate written product agreement states otherwise.
2.2 Minimum balances
- Savings minimum monthly average balance: ₹5,000 urban; ₹2,000 semi-urban/rural.
- Current account minimum balance: ₹25,000.
2.3 Service hours and access
- Phone banking 24/7.
- Branches Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM; closed public holidays.
- Digital sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity; digital banking locks after 5 failed login attempts for 30 minutes.

3. Master fee schedule (summary)
- Savings interest: 3.5% per annum (credited as per account terms).
- ATM usage:
  - NovaBank ATMs: 5 free monthly transactions, then ₹20 each.
  - Other-bank ATMs: 3 free monthly transactions, then ₹25 each.
  - Daily ATM withdrawal limit: ₹50,000.
- Debit card:
  - Debit card annual fee: ₹250, waived first year.
  - Debit card replacement fee: ₹200.
- Credit card:
  - Credit card revolving interest: 3.5% per month (42% APR).
  - Credit-card billing cycle: 30 days; minimum payment is 5% of statement balance or ₹250, whichever is higher, plus applicable interest, fees, and over-limit amounts.
- Electronic transfers:
  - IMPS: instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction.
  - NEFT: batch processing, within 2 hours, ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab, 24/7.
  - RTGS: real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000, ₹25–₹50 by amount slab, 24/7.
- Personal loans:
  - Personal loan interest: 10.5%–16% per annum by credit profile.
  - Personal loan tenure: 12–60 months.
  - Personal loan processing fee: 1.5% of loan amount.
- Account closure:
  - Processing time: 7 working days.
  - No fee if account older than 12 months.
  - ₹500 if closed within 12 months.
- KYC re-verification:
  - Every 2 years for individual accounts; every 1 year for high-risk category accounts.
- Other operational timings and service levels:
  - New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period.
  - Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000; branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.
  - ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification.
  - Fraud reported within 24 hours of discovery receives zero customer liability, subject to investigation and customer security compliance.
  - Phone support target response is within 60 seconds; secure-message response within 2 working days.
  - Complaints: acknowledgment within 24 hours; resolution within 7 working days; escalate to grievance officer if unresolved after 15 working days.

4. Applicability and examples
4.1 Account minimums and interest
- If your savings account is in an urban branch, maintain Savings minimum monthly average balance: ₹5,000 urban; ₹2,000 semi-urban/rural applies to semi-urban/rural branches. Falling below may trigger service charges per account terms.
- Interest on savings is paid at Savings interest: 3.5% per annum.
4.2 ATM and card fees
- You get 5 free NOVABank ATM withdrawals per month; on the 6th at a NovaBank ATM we charge ₹20. For other-bank ATMs, your first 3 withdrawals each month are free; after that we charge ₹25 per transaction.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit is ₹50,000; attempted excess withdrawals are declined.
- Debit card annual fee: ₹250, waived first year. A lost or damaged card replacement costs ₹200.
4.3 Electronic transfers
- IMPS is instant and available 24/7 at ₹5 per transaction.
- NEFT runs as batch processing (within 2 hours) and fees are ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab; available 24/7.
- RTGS is real-time for transfers ≥ ₹2,00,000 with fees of ₹25–₹50 by amount slab; available 24/7.
- New beneficiaries added in internet/mobile banking are subject to a 30-minute cooling period before funds can be sent.
- Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000 overall; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000.
4.4 Loans and charges
- Personal loan interest ranges from 10.5%–16% per annum depending on credit profile; tenure offered is 12–60 months. Processing fee is 1.5% of the loan amount and is collected at sanction disbursement unless otherwise agreed.

5. How fees are applied, collected, and refunded
5.1 Charging and notification
- Fees are debited to the primary account on the date of service or at monthly cycle close, and will appear on statements.
- For unclear or unexpected charges, contact phone banking (24/7) or send a secure message; target response is within 2 working days.
5.2 Refunds and failed transactions
- ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification.
- For contested transfers or duplicate charges, NovaBank will investigate and refund if appropriate per investigation results.
5.3 Fraud handling
- Report suspected fraud within 24 hours of discovery. Cases reported within 24 hours receive zero customer liability, subject to investigation and customer security compliance.

6. Customer responsibilities and steps
6.1 Preventive actions
- Keep login credentials and cards secure. After 5 failed login attempts, digital banking locks for 30 minutes.
- Log out after use; sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity.
6.2 To dispute a fee or charge
- Step 1: Contact phone banking (24/7) or visit a branch during branch hours with ID.
- Step 2: NovaBank acknowledges the complaint within 24 hours and aims to resolve it within 7 working days.
- Step 3: If unresolved after 15 working days, escalate to the grievance officer and, if required, to an independent external forum.

7. Escalation, service levels and final remedies
- Complaints acknowledged within 24 hours; resolution within 7 working days.
- Escalate to the grievance officer if unresolved after 15 working days; final escalation can include an external banking-ombudsman-style forum where applicable.
- Phone support target response is within 60 seconds; secure-message response within 2 working days; branch service is handled during branch hours.
