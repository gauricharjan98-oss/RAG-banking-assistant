# Current Account Policy

1. Purpose
1.1 This document explains NovaBank current-account eligibility, minimum balances, overdraft basics, required documents, operating rules, fees, and customer responsibilities. It also covers linked services (cards, transfers, loans) and complaint/escalation steps.

2. Eligibility
2.1 Individual accounts
- Indian residents and NRIs (subject to NRI account rules) may open current accounts if they meet KYC and minimum-balance requirements.
- KYC re-verification: every 2 years for individual accounts; every 1 year for high-risk category accounts.

2.2 Business accounts
- Sole proprietorships, partnerships, LLPs, private limited companies and other registered entities may open current accounts on submission of entity documents and authorized signatory KYC.
- Business accounts classified high-risk follow annual KYC re-verification.

2.3 Required Documents
- Individuals: proof of identity, proof of address, PAN, photograph, and any income/occupation documents requested.
- Businesses: Certificate of incorporation/registration, partnership deed/MOA/AOA as applicable, GST registration (if applicable), board resolution or authorization for signatories, and KYC for all authorized signatories.
- NovaBank may request additional documents for compliance and risk assessment.

3. Balances, interest and fees
3.1 Minimum balances
- Current account minimum balance: ₹25,000. Maintain the minimum to avoid penalties and to qualify for certain services.
- For customers who also hold savings accounts, Savings minimum monthly average balance: ₹5,000 urban; ₹2,000 semi-urban/rural.

3.2 Interest
- Savings interest: 3.5% per annum. Current accounts do not ordinarily earn savings interest unless an agreed product states otherwise.

3.3 Account closure
- Account closure: 7 working days processing time.
- No fee if account older than 12 months; ₹500 if closed within 12 months.

4. Overdrafts, loans and related charges
4.1 Overdraft basics
- Overdraft facility, if granted, is an agreed limit subject to credit assessment, collateral and terms. Interest and charges are specified in the overdraft sanction letter.

4.2 Personal loans (for eligible customers)
- Personal loan interest: 10.5%–16% per annum by credit profile.
- Personal loan tenure: 12–60 months.
- Personal loan processing fee: 1.5% of loan amount.

5. Payments, transfers and limits
5.1 Immediate and bulk channels
- IMPS: instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction.
- NEFT: batch processing, within 2 hours, ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab, 24/7.
- RTGS: real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000, ₹25–₹50 by amount slab, 24/7.

5.2 Limits and cooling
- New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period before transfers are allowed.
- Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000; branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.
- Credit-card billing cycle is 30 days; minimum payment is 5% of statement balance or ₹250, whichever is higher, plus applicable interest, fees, and over-limit amounts.
- Credit card revolving interest: 3.5% per month (42% APR).

6. ATM, cards and transaction charges
6.1 ATM usage
- ATM: 5 free monthly transactions at NovaBank ATMs, then ₹20 each; other-bank ATMs 3 free monthly, then ₹25 each.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: ₹50,000.
- ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification.

6.2 Debit cards
- Debit card annual fee: ₹250, waived first year.
- Debit card replacement fee: ₹200.

6.3 Security and fraud
- Fraud reported within 24 hours of discovery receives zero customer liability, subject to investigation and customer security compliance.

7. Account operation and security measures
7.1 Digital banking
- Digital banking locks after 5 failed login attempts for 30 minutes; sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity.
- Sessions auto-logout and multi-factor authentication are enforced; customers must keep credentials confidential.

7.2 Branch and phone support
- Support: phone banking 24/7; branches Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM; closed public holidays.
- Phone support target response is within 60 seconds; secure-message response within 2 working days; branch service is handled during branch hours.

8. Customer responsibilities
- Maintain the applicable minimum balance and provide updated KYC documents when requested.
- Report unauthorized transactions, lost/stolen cards, or suspicious activity immediately via phone banking or branch.
- Observe beneficiary cooling periods and transfer limits; confirm beneficiary details before initiating transfers.
- Pay minimum credit-card payments on time to avoid interest and penalties.

9. Complaints, resolution and escalation
- Complaints: acknowledgment within 24 hours; resolution within 7 working days.
- If a complaint is unresolved after 15 working days, escalate to the grievance officer.
- Final complaint escalation goes to the grievance officer after 15 working days, followed by an independent banking-ombudsman-style external forum where applicable.
- Securely retain transaction receipts and communications to support investigations.

10. Practical steps
10.1 To open a current account
- Gather required documents (see 2.3), visit a branch or start digital onboarding where available, complete KYC and sign account mandate, deposit minimum balance of ₹25,000.
10.2 To set up transfers and cards
- Add beneficiaries and wait 30 minutes cooling period before transferring. Order debit card (annual fee ₹250, waived first year). Set mobile-app limits as needed.
10.3 To close an account
- Request closure via branch or secure message. Account closure: 7 working days. Fee rules apply based on account age as noted in 3.3.
