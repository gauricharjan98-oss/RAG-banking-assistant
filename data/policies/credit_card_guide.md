# Credit Card Guide

1. Purpose
1.1 This guide explains eligibility, how the 30-day billing cycle works, interest and minimum payment rules, rewards, and how to request credit limit increases for NovaBank credit cards. It also covers practical steps, customer responsibilities, and escalation/safety notes relevant to cardholders.

2. Eligibility
2.1 Basic eligibility
- Applicants must be Indian residents aged 18–70 with valid KYC. KYC re-verification: every 2 years for individual accounts; every 1 year for high-risk category accounts.
- You should hold a NovaBank savings or current account or provide primary income documentation if applying without a NovaBank deposit account.

2.2 Account minimums (if linking a NovaBank account)
- Savings minimum monthly average balance: ₹5,000 urban; ₹2,000 semi-urban/rural.
- Current account minimum balance: ₹25,000.

2.3 Required documents
- Proof of identity, proof of address, PAN, recent photograph, and income proof for credit assessment.
- Additional documents may be requested for higher limits or if assessed as high risk.

3. Applying and practical steps
3.1 How to apply
- Complete the online or branch application and submit KYC and income documents.
- New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period (for card-related fund transfers).

3.2 Verification and activation
- We verify documents and credit profile; typical processing times vary by application complexity.
- If you link a NovaBank account, standard account rules apply (see section 2.2).

4. Billing cycle and statements
4.1 Billing period and statement delivery
- Credit-card billing cycle is 30 days; minimum payment is 5% of statement balance or ₹250, whichever is higher, plus applicable interest, fees, and over-limit amounts.
- Statements are delivered monthly by email and available in internet/mobile banking.

4.2 Payment types and timings
- Payments via IMPS are instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction.
- NEFT is batch processing, within 2 hours, ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab, 24/7.
- RTGS is real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000, ₹25–₹50 by amount slab, 24/7.
- Branch payments follow branch hours (Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM; closed public holidays).
- Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000; branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.

5. Interest and charges
5.1 Revolving balance interest
- Credit card revolving interest: 3.5% per month (42% APR).
- Interest applies on any part of the statement balance not paid by the payment due date and on cash advances from the transaction date.

5.2 Other fees and charges
- Late payment fees and other service charges may apply; these are shown on your schedule of charges at application and on statements.
- Over-limit amounts attract fees and are included when calculating the minimum payment.

6. Minimum payment and consequences
6.1 Minimum payment rule
- Credit-card billing cycle is 30 days; minimum payment is 5% of statement balance or ₹250, whichever is higher, plus applicable interest, fees, and over-limit amounts.

6.2 Practical implications
- Paying only the minimum extends the time to repay and increases total interest due (see section 5).
- If you miss payments repeatedly, we may restrict transactions, charge additional fees, or report payment behavior to credit information agencies.

7. Rewards and redemption
7.1 How rewards work
- Eligible transactions earn reward points or cashback based on the card variant. Points are credited as per the rewards schedule published in your card terms.
- Rewards have expiry and redemption rules published in internet banking and statements.

7.2 Using rewards
- Redeem points for statement credit, merchandise, travel or partner offers as allowed by your card variant. Redemption channels include internet banking, mobile app, and branch.

8. Credit limit and increases
8.1 Initial limit
- Initial limits are set after income and credit assessment at account opening.

8.2 Requesting an increase
- To request a limit increase, apply through internet banking, mobile app or branch with updated income documents. We may re-check KYC and credit history.

8.3 When increases are granted
- Increases are approved based on payment history, credit score and relationship with NovaBank. Higher increases may require additional documents and a fresh KYC where applicable (KYC re-verification rules apply).

9. Customer responsibilities and safety
9.1 Card security
- Keep your card and PIN secure. Report lost/stolen cards immediately.
- Digital banking locks after 5 failed login attempts for 30 minutes; sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity.

9.2 Reporting fraud and liability
- Report suspected fraud immediately by phone or secure message. Fraud reported within 24 hours of discovery receives zero customer liability, subject to investigation and customer security compliance.
- ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification.

10. Disputes, complaints and escalation
10.1 Support and response times
- Support: phone banking 24/7; branches Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM; closed public holidays.
- Phone support target response is within 60 seconds; secure-message response within 2 working days; branch service is handled during branch hours.

10.2 Complaints handling
- Complaints: acknowledgment within 24 hours; resolution within 7 working days; escalate to grievance officer if unresolved after 15 working days.
- If unresolved after escalation, you may take the matter to the independent banking-ombudsman-style external forum where applicable.

11. Practical checklists
11.1 When you receive your card
- Activate via phone or mobile app, change the PIN, and sign the card immediately.
- Verify your statement cycle date and set payment reminders.

11.2 Before requesting a limit increase
- Ensure on-time payments for at least 6 months, provide updated income proof, and complete any pending KYC re-verification.

12. Additional notes
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: ₹50,000.
- ATM: 5 free monthly transactions at NovaBank ATMs, then ₹20 each; other-bank ATMs 3 free monthly, then ₹25 each.
- Debit card annual fee: ₹250, waived first year; Debit card replacement fee: ₹200.
- Phone support target response is within 60 seconds; secure-message response within 2 working days.
