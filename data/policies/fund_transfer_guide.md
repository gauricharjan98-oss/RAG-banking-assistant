# Fund Transfer Guide

1. Purpose
1.1 This guide explains how to move funds from your NovaBank account, the available transfer channels, beneficiary setup rules, the 30-minute cooling requirement for new beneficiaries, daily limits, and security controls. It gives step-by-step actions you can take, your responsibilities, and escalation steps if something goes wrong.

2. Eligibility and account basics
2.1 Eligibility
- Any NovaBank account holder in good standing can use digital and branch transfer services.
- Account-related minimums and related figures:
  - Savings minimum monthly average balance: ₹5,000 urban; ₹2,000 semi-urban/rural.
  - Savings interest: 3.5% per annum.
  - Current account minimum balance: ₹25,000.
- KYC re-verification: every 2 years for individual accounts; every 1 year for high-risk category accounts.

2.2 Related service notes (for reference)
- Debit card annual fee: ₹250, waived first year. Debit card replacement fee: ₹200.
- Credit-card revolving interest: 3.5% per month (42% APR).
- ATM: 5 free monthly transactions at NovaBank ATMs, then ₹20 each; other-bank ATMs 3 free monthly, then ₹25 each.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: ₹50,000.
- Account closure: 7 working days; no fee if account older than 12 months; ₹500 if closed within 12 months.

3. Transfer channels and charges
3.1 Internal (same-bank) transfers
- Instant and typically processed immediately within our systems. No external network fee applies.

3.2 IMPS
- IMPS: instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction.

3.3 NEFT
- NEFT: batch processing, within 2 hours, ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab, 24/7.

3.4 RTGS
- RTGS: real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000, ₹25–₹50 by amount slab, 24/7.

3.5 Branch-assisted transfers
- Carried out during branch hours: branches Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM; closed public holidays. Branch transfers require identity verification and follow channel rules and limits.

4. Beneficiary setup and cooling
4.1 Required details to add beneficiary
- Beneficiary name, beneficiary account number, and bank IFSC (for interbank transfers). For internal transfers, account number and beneficiary name.

4.2 Process and timing
- Steps:
  1. Log in to Internet Banking or Mobile App (or visit a branch).
  2. Go to “Add Beneficiary” and enter the required details.
  3. Authenticate the addition using OTP or device approval as prompted.
  4. Wait for the cooling period before transferring.
- New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period. After this period you can initiate transfers to the new beneficiary.

5. Daily limits and channel limits
5.1 Digital transfer limits
- Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000; branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.
- If you need transfers above these limits, visit a branch for verification and assistance.

5.2 Channel-specific considerations
- IMPS and NEFT do not have an upper limit set here beyond the daily limits and channel minimums; RTGS minimum is ₹2,00,000.
- Fees apply as listed in section 3.

6. Practical step-by-step examples
6.1 Send ₹25,000 to an external account (recommended method)
- If ≥ ₹2,00,000 use RTGS (see section 3.4). For ₹25,000:
  1. Log in to Mobile App or Internet Banking.
  2. Ensure beneficiary has completed the 30-minute cooling period.
  3. Choose IMPS for instant transfer (IMPS: instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction) or NEFT if you prefer batch processing.
  4. Enter amount and purpose, confirm with OTP, and complete transfer.
  5. Note any fee charged and check transaction status.

6.2 Add a beneficiary at a branch
- Visit branch during hours with ID. Branch staff will verify identity and enter beneficiary details. Branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.

7. Controls, locks, and security
7.1 Login and session controls
- Digital banking locks after 5 failed login attempts for 30 minutes; sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity.
- Keep passwords, OTPs, and device access secure. Do not share credentials.

7.2 Failed ATM and disputed transactions
- ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification.
- If you detect fraud, report it immediately (see section 8 for escalation). Fraud reported within 24 hours of discovery receives zero customer liability, subject to investigation and customer security compliance.

7.3 Monitoring and alerts
- Enable SMS and email alerts to track transfers. Review statements regularly and report unrecognized transfers promptly.

8. Customer responsibilities and support
8.1 Your responsibilities
- Provide correct beneficiary account and IFSC details.
- Confirm beneficiary identity and details before initiating transfers.
- Maintain updated KYC and contact details to receive OTPs and alerts.
- Report lost/stolen cards, unauthorized access, or suspected fraud immediately.

8.2 Support and response targets
- Phone banking 24/7; phone support target response is within 60 seconds.
- Secure-message response within 2 working days.
- Branch service is handled during branch hours.
- Complaints: acknowledgment within 24 hours; resolution within 7 working days; escalate to grievance officer if unresolved after 15 working days. Final complaint escalation goes to the grievance officer after 15 working days, followed by an independent banking-ombudsman-style external forum where applicable.

9. Escalation steps
- First contact: Phone banking or secure message with transaction reference.
- If unresolved beyond 7 working days: follow up and request escalation.
- If unresolved after 15 working days: escalate to the grievance officer (branch or digital channel will provide contact details) and then to an external forum where applicable.

10. Other product fees and notes (quick reference)
- Personal loan interest: 10.5%–16% per annum by credit profile. Personal loan tenure: 12–60 months. Personal loan processing fee: 1.5% of loan amount.
- Credit-card billing cycle is 30 days; minimum payment is 5% of statement balance or ₹250, whichever is higher, plus applicable interest, fees, and over-limit amounts.
