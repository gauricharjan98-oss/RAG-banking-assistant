# KYC Requirements

1. Purpose
1.1 This document explains NovaBank's Know Your Customer (KYC) requirements for opening and maintaining accounts, how identity and address categories are treated, the re-KYC schedule and triggers, and generic handling for NRI and foreign-national customers. It gives practical steps you must follow and how to escalate issues.

2. Eligibility
2.1 Individuals and entities who wish to open accounts with NovaBank must complete KYC to the satisfaction of the bank. Different account types and risk profiles determine the level of documentation required.
2.2 Savings minimum monthly average balance: ₹5,000 urban; ₹2,000 semi-urban/rural.
2.3 Current account minimum balance: ₹25,000.

2.1 Required Documents (for individual customers)
- Proof of identity (POI): government photo ID such as passport, driver's license, national identity card (PAN, Aadhaar, where applicable), or other government-issued photo ID.
- Proof of address (POA): recent utility bill, bank statement, passport, voter ID, rental agreement, or similar document showing current address.
- Recent passport-sized photograph.
- PAN card or tax identifier where required for tax reporting and high-value accounts.
- Additional documents for special categories (see section 5).

3. Identity and Address Categories
3.1 Identity categories
- Primary photo ID: passport, driver's license, national identity card.
- Secondary ID (if primary not available): PAN, voter ID, or notarized affidavit with two supporting IDs.
3.2 Address categories
- Official proof: utility bill, bank statement, passport, voter ID.
- Alternative proof: rental agreement, letter from employer or government body, or sworn affidavit.
3.3 Documentary and digital verification
- NovaBank accepts certified physical copies at branches and verified digital copies via secure banking channels. The bank may perform remote video verification where allowed and technically feasible.
3.4 Updating address
- To change your registered address, submit a valid POA and ID. Address updates may take up to 2 working days to reflect in most systems after verification.

4. Re-KYC and Triggers
4.1 Re-KYC frequency
- KYC re-verification: every 2 years for individual accounts; every 1 year for high-risk category accounts.
4.2 Triggers requiring earlier re-KYC
- Change of name, address, nationality, or occupation.
- High-value or unusual transactions.
- Non-activity or dormant account reviews.
- Regulatory alerts or sanctions screening hits.
- Customer request for higher transaction limits or new products.
4.3 Practical steps for re-KYC
- You will receive notification by secure message, SMS, or email. Provide updated documents within the timeframe specified (typically 30 days unless otherwise stated).
- Failure to complete re-KYC may lead to temporary restrictions on transactions until verification is complete.

5. NRI and Foreign-National Accounts (generic handling)
5.1 Types and documentation
- NRIs: provide valid passport, overseas residence proof, visa/OCI/PIO details where applicable, and Indian tax identifier (PAN or tax documentation) if required.
- Foreign nationals resident in India: provide passport, valid Indian visa/residence permit, and local address proof.
- Non-resident accounts may require additional tax and source-of-funds documentation.
5.2 Practical steps
- Open NRI/foreign-national accounts at designated branches or via the bank's secure onboarding flow. Provide originals for verification when requested.
- Account usage and product availability may differ; NovaBank will explain transactional limits and remit rules at onboarding.
5.3 Generic note
- NovaBank will follow internal due diligence to determine acceptable documents and may ask for translations or notarization where required.

6. Customer Responsibilities and Practical Steps
6.1 How to submit KYC
- In branch: bring originals and photocopies. Branches operate Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM; closed public holidays.
- Digital: upload clear scanned or photographed documents via the mobile app or secure online portal. Mobile-app uploads should follow on-screen instructions.
- Phone: call phone banking 24/7 for guidance; target response within 60 seconds.
6.2 Timelines and actions
- Provide requested documents promptly. New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period after activation.
- Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000; branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.
6.3 Security responsibilities
- Keep login credentials confidential. Digital banking locks after 5 failed login attempts for 30 minutes. Sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity.
- Report suspected fraud immediately. Fraud reported within 24 hours of discovery receives zero customer liability, subject to investigation and customer security compliance.

7. Escalation, Safety Notes, and Service Targets
7.1 If you face issues submitting KYC
- First contact phone banking 24/7 or visit a branch during branch hours.
- Secure-message response target is within 2 working days.
7.2 Complaints and resolution
- Complaints acknowledgment within 24 hours; resolution within 7 working days.
- If unresolved after 15 working days, escalate to the grievance officer. Final complaint escalation goes to the grievance officer after 15 working days, followed by an independent banking-ombudsman-style external forum where applicable.
7.3 ATM and card safety
- ATM: 5 free monthly transactions at NovaBank ATMs, then ₹20 each; other-bank ATMs 3 free monthly, then ₹25 each. Daily ATM withdrawal limit: ₹50,000.
- ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification.
- Debit card annual fee: ₹250, waived first year. Debit card replacement fee: ₹200.
- Credit-card billing cycle is 30 days; minimum payment is 5% of statement balance or ₹250, whichever is higher, plus applicable interest, fees, and over-limit amounts. Credit card revolving interest: 3.5% per month (42% APR).

8. Related Product Figures (quick reference)
- Savings interest: 3.5% per annum.
- Personal loan interest: 10.5%–16% per annum by credit profile.
- Personal loan tenure: 12–60 months.
- Personal loan processing fee: 1.5% of loan amount.
- IMPS: instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction.
- NEFT: batch processing, within 2 hours, ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab, 24/7.
- RTGS: real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000, ₹25–₹50 by amount slab, 24/7.
- Account closure: 7 working days; no fee if account older than 12 months; ₹500 if closed within 12 months.

9. Contact and help
- For KYC questions, use phone banking 24/7 or visit your nearest branch Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM. If you need faster assistance with restricted accounts, request escalation when you contact phone banking.
