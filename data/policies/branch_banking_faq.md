# Branch Banking FAQ

1. Purpose
1.1 This document answers common questions about branch hours, branch-vs-digital services, appointments, and how NovaBank handles public holidays. It gives practical steps, customer responsibilities, and escalation/safety notes for everyday branch and digital interactions.

2. Branch hours and availability
2.1 Branch hours: Branches are open Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–4:30 PM; closed public holidays. Branch service is handled during branch hours.
2.2 Phone and digital alternatives: Phone banking is available 24/7. For many payments and checks you can use the mobile app or internet banking outside branch hours.

3. Branch vs digital — what to use when
3.1 Recommended for branch visits
- Complex services that require in-person verification (e.g., some account openings, notarised forms).
- Large cash withdrawals or deposits beyond ATM practical limits.
- Account closure (see processing time and fee rules).
- Loan document signing and appraisal visits.

3.2 Recommended for digital channels
- Immediate transfers and routine bill payments: IMPS is instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction.
- NEFT for transfers: NEFT is batch processing, within 2 hours, ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab, 24/7.
- RTGS for large urgent payments: RTGS is real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000, ₹25–₹50 by amount slab, 24/7.
- Routine balance checks, mini-statements, statements download, and card controls via the app.

3.3 Limits and timings to note
- New fund-transfer beneficiaries have a 30-minute cooling period before you can send funds to them through digital channels.
- Daily digital fund-transfer limit is ₹5,00,000; mobile-app limit is ₹2,00,000; branch-assisted transfers follow account verification and channel limits.
- Digital banking locks after 5 failed login attempts for 30 minutes; sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity.

4. Appointments and walk-ins
4.1 Booking an appointment
- How: Book by phone banking (24/7) or via the secure-message option in the mobile app or website. Branch staff will confirm available slots during branch hours.
- Use appointments for account opening, loan interviews, large cash transactions, and dispute meetings.
- Bring ID and any required forms to your appointment; late arrivals may be asked to reschedule.

4.2 Walk-ins
- Walk-ins are welcome but service priority is given to appointment holders during busy periods. If you arrive without an appointment, we will serve you as capacity allows during branch hours.

5. Public holidays and service continuity
5.1 Branches are closed on public holidays.
5.2 Digital and phone alternatives on public holidays
- Phone banking 24/7 for assistance.
- IMPS is instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction.
- NEFT is batch processing, within 2 hours, ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab, 24/7.
- RTGS is real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000, ₹25–₹50 by amount slab, 24/7.
5.3 Appointments cancelled due to public holidays
- If a scheduled appointment falls on a public holiday, the branch will contact you to reschedule. You can also rebook via phone banking or the app.

6. Fees, limits and common charges (summary)
- Savings minimum monthly average balance: ₹5,000 urban; ₹2,000 semi-urban/rural.
- Savings interest: 3.5% per annum.
- Current account minimum balance: ₹25,000.
- ATM: 5 free monthly transactions at NovaBank ATMs, then ₹20 each; other-bank ATMs 3 free monthly, then ₹25 each.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: ₹50,000.
- Debit card annual fee: ₹250, waived first year.
- Debit card replacement fee: ₹200.
- Credit card revolving interest: 3.5% per month (42% APR).
- Credit-card billing cycle is 30 days; minimum payment is 5% of statement balance or ₹250, whichever is higher, plus applicable interest, fees, and over-limit amounts.
- Personal loan interest: 10.5%–16% per annum by credit profile.
- Personal loan tenure: 12–60 months.
- Personal loan processing fee: 1.5% of loan amount.
- IMPS: instant, 24/7, ₹5 per transaction.
- NEFT: batch processing, within 2 hours, ₹2.50–₹25 by amount slab, 24/7.
- RTGS: real-time, minimum ₹2,00,000, ₹25–₹50 by amount slab, 24/7.
- Account closure: 7 working days; no fee if account older than 12 months; ₹500 if closed within 12 months.

7. Problems, complaints, and security
7.1 ATM and card issues
- If an ATM transaction fails or cash not dispensed, report it immediately via phone banking or the branch. ATM failed-transaction refunds are completed within 5 working days after verification.
- For lost/stolen cards: block the card immediately via the app or phone banking. Debit card replacement fee: ₹200 (annual fee ₹250, waived first year noted above).
7.2 Fraud and unauthorized transactions
- Report fraud within 24 hours of discovery. Fraud reported within 24 hours of discovery receives zero customer liability, subject to investigation and customer security compliance.
7.3 Complaint handling and escalation
- Complaints: acknowledgment within 24 hours; resolution within 7 working days. If unresolved after 15 working days escalate to the grievance officer. Final complaint escalation goes to the grievance officer after 15 working days, followed by an independent banking-ombudsman-style external forum where applicable.
- Phone support target response is within 60 seconds; secure-message response within 2 working days.

8. Customer responsibilities and documents
8.1 KYC and periodic checks
- KYC re-verification: every 2 years for individual accounts; every 1 year for high-risk category accounts. Bring valid ID and address proof when requested.
8.2 What to bring for common services
- Account opening: valid photo ID, address proof, recent photograph, PAN or Form 60 as applicable.
- Loan application: identity, address, income proof, and any property documentation requested.
8.3 Keep records
- Retain receipts and transaction references until the matter is fully resolved. For failed transactions or suspected fraud, immediate reporting by you speeds resolution.
